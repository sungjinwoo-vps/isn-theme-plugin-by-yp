# Elementor Guide

- Use Elementor Free from the official WordPress repository.
- Do not install nulled or unofficial Elementor Pro.
- InfoSecNexus registers Header, Footer, Single, and Archive theme locations.
- Native theme templates render when Elementor templates are absent.
- Toolkit widgets are under the `InfoSecNexus` Elementor category.
- Elementor Pro Theme Builder compatibility is passive and depends on a legal Elementor Pro installation.
